# 01 — Product Requirements Document (PRD)
**Project:** ARTS APPAREL — Online Men's Clothing Store
**Version:** 1.0  |  **Language of document:** English (UI copy examples include Urdu)

---

## 1. Purpose & Goals

ARTS APPAREL is a men's clothing brand based in Sadiqabad, Pakistan. This project is a **full online store** where customers browse and buy the brand's clothing, and where the owner and staff manage products, orders, payments and content from an **Admin Panel**.

**Goals**
1. Let customers anywhere in Pakistan browse, order and track clothing from a phone in under 3 minutes.
2. Give the owner one place to manage catalog, stock, orders, payments, coupons, delivery and content without any coding.
3. Use **AI (Cloudflare Workers AI, free plan)** where it genuinely saves time for customers (shopping assistant, smart search, size help) and for staff (product descriptions, translation, tags).
4. Premium, animated, mobile-first brand experience matching the ARTS APPAREL logo (black, red, white).

**Success measures:** checkout completes without login; admin can add a product with variants and images in under 5 minutes; owner sees today's orders and low stock at a glance; AI features never break the site when the free daily limit is reached.

**Trust & content**
- Reviews & ratings (only verified buyers who received the product; moderated by staff).

- **English / Urdu** language switch with full RTL layout for Urdu; language choice remembered.
- Floating WhatsApp chat button on every page.
- SEO: per-page title and meta description, Open Graph tags, sitemap-ready routes, product JSON-LD schema, fast loading, lazy images.

### 4.2 AI Features (Cloudflare Workers AI — free plan, ~10,000 neurons/day)

All AI calls go through **server-side endpoints on Cloudflare** (never directly from the browser to a model, never with keys in the frontend). Every feature has a **graceful fallback** when the daily limit is reached or the AI is unavailable. See 02_TRD.md §9 for limits and endpoint contracts.

| # | Feature | Who | What it does | Fallback when limit reached |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **AI Shopping Assistant** (chat widget) | Customer | Answers questions ("shadi ke liye kya pehnu?", "winter jacket under 8000") in English/Urdu/Roman Urdu, recommends **real products from the catalog** with links, explains size/delivery/payment/returns using store settings | Shows "Aaj ka AI limit poora ho gaya" + WhatsApp button |
| A2 | **Natural-Language Search** | Customer | Turns a sentence into structured filters (category, color, size, max price) and runs the normal product query | Plain keyword search |
| A3 | **Smart Size Recommendation** | Customer | Height + weight + fit preference → recommended size from the `size_charts` table. **Rule-based first (no AI cost)**; AI only writes a short friendly explanation | Rule-based result without explanation |
| A4 | **Product Description Generator** | Admin/staff | From name, category, fabric, bullet points → English + Urdu description in the brand tone | Manual typing |
| A5 | **Translate EN ↔ UR** | Admin/staff | Translates product name/description/banner text | Manual typing |
| A6 | **SEO helper & auto-tags** | Admin/staff | Generates `seo_title`, `seo_description`, search `tags` from the product data | Manual typing |
| A7 | **Review Summary** | Customer (read) | Short summary of approved reviews on a product; generated on demand by staff or automatically when review count changes, then **cached in `review_summaries`** (not regenerated per visitor) | Hide summary block |

Rule for all AI text: never invent products, prices, stock or policies — answers must come from catalog/settings data passed to the model.

## 5. Business Rules

1. **Prices are server-trusted.** The browser sends only product/variant ids and quantities; the database function recalculates prices, coupon discount and delivery charge. A customer can never set their own price.
2. **Stock is per variant** (size + color). Placing an order decrements stock atomically; if stock is insufficient the order fails with a clear message. Cancelling an order restores stock.
3. **Order number format:** `ARTS-000001` (zero-padded sequence). Shown to the customer and used for tracking.
4. **Order status flow:** `pending → confirmed → processing → shipped → delivered`; side exits `cancelled` (before shipped) and `returned` (after delivered).
5. **Payment status:** `pending` (COD default) · `awaiting_verification` (proof uploaded) · `paid` · `failed` · `refunded`. COD orders become `paid` when delivered and cash is received (staff marks it).
6. **Customer cancellation** only while status is `pending` or `confirmed`. After that the customer must contact the store (WhatsApp button shown).
7. **Returns:** request allowed only for `delivered` orders within `return_window_days` (default 7) of delivery.
8. **Coupons:** valid if active, within dates, subtotal ≥ minimum, usage limit not exceeded. One coupon per order. `percent` coupons respect `max_discount`.
9. **Delivery charge:** looked up by city; if city not listed use the default charge; if subtotal ≥ `free_delivery_threshold` (when set) the charge is 0.
10. **Reviews:** one review per user per product, only after a delivered order containing that product; hidden until approved.
11. **Guest orders** have `user_id = null`. When a guest later signs up with a verified email matching the order's email, those orders may be linked to the new account.
12. **Phone validation:** Pakistani mobile `03XXXXXXXXX` (11 digits) or `+923XXXXXXXXX`; store normalized as `03XXXXXXXXX`.
13. **Low stock** = variant stock ≤ `low_stock_threshold` (default 5); shown on dashboard and highlighted in product list. Out-of-stock variants are shown but not selectable.

## 6. Functional Requirements (summary)
- FR1 Catalog browsing with filters/sort/search on all devices.
- FR2 Cart persists in browser storage; validated against live stock/prices at checkout.
- FR3 Guest and registered checkout with the four payment methods above (card disabled).
- FR4 Role-based admin panel (owner/staff) protected by Supabase Auth + RLS.
- FR5 Image upload to storage (product images, banners, payment proofs).
- FR6 English/Urdu bilingual content fields (`*_en`, `*_ur`) with RTL layout.
- FR7 AI endpoints with per-visitor and global daily limits and fallbacks.
- FR8 Printable invoice and packing slip.
- FR9 Demo mode: when Supabase environment variables are missing, the app runs with built-in sample data and a visible "DEMO MODE" badge (so the UI can be tested in AI Studio before the database exists).

## 7. Non-Functional Requirements
- **Mobile-first** (most customers use phones on mobile data): fast first load, lazy-loaded images, route-level code splitting, target Lighthouse performance ≥ 85 on mobile.
- **Security:** RLS on every table, no secrets in frontend/GitHub, server-side price/stock validation, input validation, rate limits on AI and order placement, admin routes protected both in UI and database.
- **Accessibility:** keyboard focus visible, sufficient contrast, alt text, labels on all inputs, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected.
- **Reliability:** AI failures or quota exhaustion never block browsing or checkout.
- **Cost:** Cloudflare free tier + Supabase free tier at launch; AI usage hard-capped below the free neuron allowance.
- **Maintainability:** typed service layer, consistent table/column names identical to 05_Backend_Schema.md.

## 8. Scope

**In scope (launch):** everything in §4 and §5.
**Out of scope for now (design so they can be added later):** online card payment gateway, courier API integration/auto-tracking, SMS/WhatsApp automatic notifications, email notifications, abandoned-cart reminders, loyalty points, multi-warehouse inventory, multi-currency, mobile app.
